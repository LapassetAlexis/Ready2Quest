# Benchmark: collection systems that don't depend on battles

## 1. WoW

**Pet Journal (retail).**
- **Filters:** collected / not collected, favourites, 10 pet families, sources (drop, quest, vendor, profession, wild pet, achievement, world event, promotion, TCG, shop, discovery, trading post), plus sort by name / level / rarity / type. The search box matches names and abilities.
- **Rules:** you can hold at most 3 of each species. Quality runs poor → common → uncommon → rare, and Battle-Stones upgrade a pet to rare.
- **Breeds:** 10 stat breeds (P/P, S/S, H/B…, IDs 3–12, +10 for the female form). Only a few exist per species ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Pet_Battle_System), [WarcraftPets](https://www2.warcraftpets.com/wow-pet-battles/breeds)). The game never shows them. The BattlePetBreedID addon adds the breed to tooltips, and that alone created a "breed hunting" metagame.
- **Trading:** tradable pets can be caged and sold on the AH.
- **Why it works:** the cap of 3 keeps duplicates scarce and meaningful. The hidden breed adds depth at no UI cost. Caging turns duplicates into gold rather than clutter.
- **Complaints:** no breed shown natively, the filter dropdown is deep and nested, and there is no bulk caging.

**Rematch (addon).** It replaces the journal list:
- **Search:** a richer box (stat comparisons such as `speed>300` in recent versions, "strong vs" / "tough vs").
- **Filters:** breed, quantity (2+, 3), moveset, level not 25, plus saved favourite filters.
- **Organisation:** teams with free-text notes per team and per opponent, user tabs/groups, and a levelling queue.
- **Why it works:** presets plus notes turn a 1,500-pet list into "my working sets".

**Classic hunter pets.**
- **Families:** each has a diet (meat, fish, bread, cheese, fruit, fungus) and family abilities (Bite/Claw/Dash/Prowl/Charge/Screech…). You learn abilities by taming beasts that know a given rank.
- **Loyalty:** 6 ranks, from 1 to 6 (Rebellious → Best Friend). It rises with kills while the pet is out ([Icy Veins](https://icy-veins.com/wow-classic/hunter-dps-pets-guide), [Warcraft Tavern](https://warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/hunter-pets)).
- **Happiness:** Happy / Content / Unhappy, which scales damage 125 / 100 / 75 %. A neglected low-loyalty pet runs away.
- **Training points:** level × (loyalty − 1), so 300 at level 60 with loyalty 6. They can go negative right after taming ([wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Training_point)).
- **Why it works:** a single pet carries a history of time invested, feeding rituals and rare skins (Broken Tooth, Humar). Individuality comes from the bond, not from stats.
- **Complaints:** feeding chores and opaque numbers.

## 2. Palworld

- **Work suitabilities:** each Pal has 12 types (Kindling, Watering, Planting, Electricity, Handiwork, Gathering, Lumbering, Mining, Medicine, Cooling, Transporting, Farming) at levels 1–4/5. That gives every individual a "job identity" at the base.
- **Passive skills:** up to 4, with tiers from negative to rainbow/legendary, inherited through breeding.
- **Partner skills:** one per species, used for riding, gliders, base effects.
- **Condensing duplicates:** stars cost 4 / 8 / 12 / 24 extra copies, 48 in total in 1.0 (pre-1.0 it was 116). Each star gives +5 % HP/ATK/DEF, +20 % at 4★ ([Nodecraft](https://nodecraft.com/support/games/palworld/general/how-pal-condensation-changed-in-palworld-1-0), [wiki](https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Pal_Condensation)).
- **Why it works:** duplicates feed an existing Pal instead of piling up, and the base turns traits into visible utility.
- **Complaints:** RNG grind for passives through breeding, a Palbox that is painful to sort and lacks bulk actions, and the old 116-copy cost, which was cut after backlash.

## 3. Monster Rancher / Monster Hunter Stories (light)

- **Monster Rancher:** monsters are generated from your own CDs, so origin becomes the meaning.
- **MH Stories:** a "gene" bingo grid you transplant between monsters, giving a visible per-individual build.

## 4. Ooblets / Temtem / Cassette Beasts

- **Ooblets:** dance-offs instead of fights. You get Ooblets by winning dance-offs and growing their seeds, rare "gleamy" variants exist, and the identity is cute and domestic. Complaint: the dances get repetitive.
- **Temtem:**
  - **Luma (shiny):** wild odds are 1/10,000 since EA 0.6.12. Each Luma parent multiplies the hatch odds ×10.
  - **SVs:** 1–50 per stat, and a Luma gets ≥3 perfect ones ([wiki](https://temtem.wiki.gg/wiki/Luma_Temtem)).
  - **Traits:** 2 possible per species.
  - **Complaints:** an SV grind that is opaque without a calculator.
- **Cassette Beasts bootlegs:** alternate-type shiny-like variants. Static encounters are 2 %, rogue fusions 2 %, and fusion swarms 10 %; community sources cite 1/1000 for the overworld ([wiki](https://wiki.cassettebeasts.com/wiki/Bootleg)). Why it works: a rarity that also changes gameplay, not only colour.

## 5. Idle / gacha

- **Neko Atsume:** cats visit, but you never own them. You collect sightings and photos in the Catbook plus gifted "mementos" (one per cat). Why it works: there are no duplicates by design, and each collectible has one story object. Complaint: long waits.
- **Cookie Run: Kingdom:** duplicates become soulstones, which promote a cookie through stars and then ascension. It is a clean dupe sink, but it ties power to pulls.
- **Gacha constellations (Genshin):**
  - **Pity:** soft at 74, hard at 90, 50/50 then guaranteed; weapons 63/80 ([KQM](https://library.keqingmains.com/evidence/general-mechanics/gacha)).
  - **Cost:** C6 = 7 copies, estimated at roughly $900–2,000 ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/genshin-impact-how-much-five-star-constellation-costs-cash-primogems/)).
  - **Pay-to-win risk:** when duplicates give power, spending converts directly into strength, which makes the game unfair in shared or competitive contexts.
  - **Why pity exists:** it caps the worst-case frustration and makes outcomes plannable.

## 6. Museums and records

- **Animal Crossing NH:**
  - **Scope:** 80 fish, 80 bugs, 40 sea creatures.
  - **Availability:** each depends on month × hemisphere × hour, and the Critterpedia shows "leaving this month" warnings.
  - **Museum:** donating fills it, and Blathers gives a lore line per item.
  - **Size:** shown only as a shadow class, with no records kept.
  - **Why it works:** the calendar gives you a reason to log in, and a donation is a visible, permanent placement.
  - **Complaint:** missing a season means waiting a year.
- **Stardew Valley:** fish quality (silver/gold/iridium, perfect-catch bonus), a Collections tab with the record size per fish, museum donations (95 items, 42 artifacts + 53 minerals) with milestone rewards, and Community Center bundles. Bundles give duplicates a destination tied to a progress goal.
- **Fishing games (Sea of Thieves, Fishing Planet, Dredge):** trophy fish, weight or size records per species, and aberrant variants. Size makes one individual brag-worthy without affecting combat.

## 7. Recurring lessons

- **Meaning per individual:** comes from origin (where and when it was caught), a record (size, quality), a bond (time invested), a job or utility, or a one-line story.
- **Duplicates:** they need a sink (condense, cage/sell, donate, bundle) or a hard cap (3).
- **Opaque hidden stats:** they spawn addons and calculators, so show them or don't add them.
- **Time-gating:** it works when it is legible (a "leaving soon" badge).

## UX patterns for dense filtering in a small fixed window

- **Toggle chips** above the grid, one row that scrolls horizontally, with an active count badge and an "×" to clear all.
- **Facet counts** on each chip or dropdown entry ("Rare (12)"). Hide or grey out zero-result facets.
- **Search tokens**: free text plus `key:value` (`size:xl`, `trait:brave`, `q>3`, `-fav`), with autocomplete and tokens rendered as removable pills.
- **Saved filters / presets** (Rematch-style), named and pinned as tabs, with built-ins such as "Duplicates", "New", "Releasable".
- **Sort**: a single control with direction, plus a secondary sort on ties, remembered per view.
- **Multi-select**: click, shift-click for a range, ctrl-click to toggle, drag lasso, and "select all matching filter" (not just what's visible).
- **Bulk bar**: appears only while something is selected, shows "N selected", and holds actions (release, sell, favourite, tag, move). Destructive actions sit at the far end.
- **Protect/lock flag**: locked items are excluded from bulk actions and from "select all", with a padlock icon. Favourites imply a lock.
- **Undo** via a toast with a timer, instead of confirm dialogs, plus a soft-delete buffer such as "recently released".
- **Dense list/grid toggle**, sticky headers, and a hover detail panel instead of opening each item.
- **New-item dot**, cleared when the item is viewed.
- **Empty-state hint** when filters return nothing ("2 filters active, clear?").
