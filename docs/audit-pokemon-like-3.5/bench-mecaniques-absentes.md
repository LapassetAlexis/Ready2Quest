# What Quest Companion lacks: Pokémon-like and collection mechanics that fit an addon

Sources: [S1] [Serebii, shiny odds in Scarlet/Violet](https://serebii.net/scarletviolet/shinypokemon.shtml), [S2] [r/ShinyPokemon odds wiki](https://reddit.birdcat.cafe/r/ShinyPokemon/wiki/odds), [S3] [Bulbapedia, Community Day](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Community%20Day), [S4] [Dittobase, Spotlight Hours](https://www.dittobase.com/pokemon-go/spotlight-hours), [S5] [Beebom, Spotlight Hour schedule (March 2026)](https://beebom.com/pokemon-go-spotlight-hour-schedule/amp/), [S6] [Bulbapedia, PokéStop Showcase](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9Stop_Showcase), [S7] [esports.gg, change to Showcase scoring](https://esports.gg/news/pokemon/niantic-introduces-big-change-to-pokestop-showcases), [S8] [Dittobase, Showcase calculator](https://www.dittobase.com/pokemon-go/showcase-calculator), [S9] [Pokémon GO Hub, Showcase rewards buffed](https://pokemongohub.net/post/news/pokemon-go-showcase-rewards-buffed/). **(U)** marks a figure from memory that I did not check.

| Mechanic | Reference game and how it works (key numbers) | Why players like it | Known complaints | Addon feasibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Evolution, forms, regional variants** | Every main game: level, item or trade triggers. Alolan and Galarian forms. Burmy changes cloak with terrain. | Long-term goal; a specimen you raised "grows up". | Evolving erases the base form; trade evolutions need a partner. | High. Forms are a data layer: one creature family seen in a different zone or continent = variant. The 3D model can't change unless a real model exists, so use tint, size or a title. |
| **Mass outbreaks / swarms** | Scarlet/Violet: an outbreak with 60+ knockouts gives about 1/1365 shiny odds, about 1/512 with the Shiny Charm and level 3 Sparkling Power [S2]. Serebii's table differs, about 1/1024 [S1]. Legends Arceus has massive outbreaks too (U). | Short-lived hotspot, chain dopamine, the "go now" feeling. | Grind, some FOMO, desync between players. | High. Pick a creature family or zone with a date seed (the same for everyone on a given day), boost shiny and quality odds after N kills. It plugs into the existing kill-chain shiny odds. |
| **Community Day / Spotlight Hour** | GO Community Day: 3 hours a month, shiny about 1/25 against about 1/500 normally [S3]. Spotlight Hour: 1 hour weekly with spawns boosted but normal shiny odds [S4]; reportedly removed in 2026, replaced by Showcases [S5] (single source). | A shared date that gets a community to log in together. | Fixed time zone, burnout, "mandatory" feel. | High for the bonus window. Spawns can't be forced, so it must be a family people can farm anywhere in Azeroth. |
| **Daily habitat bonus** | GO weekly biome and habitat rotation; Legends Arceus spawns vary by area (U). | A reason to visit other zones. | Can feel arbitrary. | High. Zone of the day plus a type bonus, all date-seeded. |
| **Time-of-day and weather spawns** | Main games: day/night species. GO weather boost, Sword/Shield Wild Area weather (U). | Immersion, "come back tonight". | Real-time gating annoys people with fixed play hours. | Medium. Time can be read (`GetGameTime`). Weather can't be read reliably in Classic (unverified), so drop it or use zone and season. Gate *bonus quality or variants*, never whether a creature can be captured. |
| **Safari Zone / Bug-Catching Contest** | Gen 2 contest: about 20 min, score = level + IVs + HP + rarity, prizes for the top 3 (U). Safari: 30 balls / 500 steps (U). | A short sprint with a rule twist. | RNG on scoring. | High. A 15-20 min timed hunt in one zone, score from the best specimen, result shared on the guild channel. |
| **Pokémon Snap / photo dex** | Snap: photos scored on pose, size, direction and other creatures in the frame, 1-4 stars per pose (U). | Skill expression, art. | Grinding hidden poses. | Medium. Real "photos" are impossible. Instead: a model viewer with the poses the creature model can play, unlocked by bond level. Cheap and cosmetic. |
| **Showcases / contests** | GO PokéStop Showcase: rank by size, now scored relative to the creature's own species [S7], score from height, weight and IVs, about 1,178 points max (community figure) [S8]. Top 3 get items [S9]. Main-game Contests: Beauty, Cool, etc. | Turns size records into competition; low stakes. | "Biggest always wins" (fixed by relative scoring), weak rewards [S9]. | High. A weekly guild showcase over addon messages: species and score in a 255-byte message, highest score at a fixed time. Needs anti-cheat (signatures are weak). Score relative to the species. |
| **Poké Pelago / Poké Jobs / expeditions** | Sun/Moon Pelago: isles, real-time timers. Sword/Shield Poké Jobs: 1 h to 24 h, XP plus items (U). | Stored creatures become useful; logout progress. | Tedium, dead menus. | High. Send 1-3 reserve specimens on a timed expedition: returns paillettes or a lore fragment, type matches give a bonus. |
| **Teams and synergies without combat** | Pokémon Masters sync pairs; Monster Hunter Palico pairings (U). | Theory-crafting. | Opaque math. | Medium. An "expedition team" or "showcase trio" with type or nature synergies. |
| **Display spaces** | HOME boxes; Palworld base; Viva Piñata garden; Animal Crossing museum (Blathers adds lore per donation). | Pride, curation. | Inventory clutter. | Medium. A museum or garden panel with 3D models on plinths, unlocked by donating a specimen. The existing showcase of 3 extends this way. |
| **Wonder Trade / GTS** | Wonder Trade: blind and instant. GTS: deposit a specimen and ask for a specific one (U). | Surprise; filling the dex. | Junk deposits; impossible GTS requests. | Medium. Guild-only async "bottle" pool. The other player must be online, because there is no server; store-and-forward through guild members is complex. |
| **Raids / community goals** | GO raids; global challenges ("catch 3 billion", U). | Belonging. | Sync and lockouts. | Medium. A guild weekly total of captures summed over guild messages. Undercounts when players are offline. |
| **Legendaries as quest lines** | Legends Arceus Nobles; GO Special Research chains (U). | A story arc with climax. | One-time, so dead afterwards. | High. Multi-step research: kill X in zone Y at night, deliver a specimen, find an object. Uses rare elites and world bosses already in the game. |
| **Alpha / titan / totem** | Legends Arceus alphas: bigger, red eyes (U). | Danger plus trophy. | Spikes in difficulty. | High. Mark elites and rare elites as "alpha" specimens with a size class beyond XXL, a halo and a title. |
| **Research levels beyond completion** | Legends Arceus research level 10 with "perfect" at 100% of tasks (U). GO medals in bronze/silver/gold/platinum. | Mastery after "caught". | Checklist fatigue. | High. Research tasks exist already: add mastery tiers per species. |
| **Seasonal track (no money)** | GO seasons; a free battle-pass ladder. | Steady pacing. | FOMO when rewards expire. | High. 4-6 week track of cosmetic tiers; past tracks stay reachable later. |
| **Login without FOMO** | Rested-XP style banking; Animal Crossing ticks over in real time. | Respect for time. | — | High. "Gifts accumulate up to 7 days", no streak reset. |
| **Lore / dex text** | Dex entries; museum plaques. | Worldbuilding. | Thin text. | High. Entries unlock by bond level and research tier. Place lore is already there. |
| **Cries / music collection** | Dex cries; jukeboxes in Animal Crossing. | Completionism. | — | High. The game's NPC sound kits for creatures can be played. The jukebox already exists, so add cries to it. |
| **Tricks as cosmetics** | Pokémon Amie/Refresh; Nintendogs tricks (U). | Attachment. | — | Medium. The follower plays a model animation on `/qc trick`, unlocked by bond level. |
| **Mount analogues** | Legends Arceus ride Pokémon; Palworld mounts. | Fantasy. | — | Low. The addon can't move the character; only a cosmetic "steed of choice" title or display. |

## Best appeal-to-cost ratio, in order
1. **Date-seeded outbreaks and a daily habitat**: reuse kill-chain odds, no network needed.
2. **Expeditions for stored specimens**: gives the reserve a use, and players come back to collect the rewards.
3. **Guild showcase weekly contest**: size records gain a stage; species-relative scoring.
4. **Legendary research quest lines** on world bosses and rare elites.
5. **Alpha variants** for elites and rares: one flag, big trophy feel.
6. **Mastery tiers and medals** on species research.
7. **Lore and cries unlocked by bond and research**: assets exist already.
8. **Timed safari / bug-catching contest** in one zone, solo or guild.

## Avoid
- **Weather-gated variants**: weather can't be read reliably (unverified), so players would see bugs.
- **Real evolution that changes models**: there are few matching models, so changing form erases the specimen's identity. Use titles and tints instead.
- **Open GTS / Wonder Trade across the realm**: there is no server; guild relay gets spoofed, messages are lost, and it invites scams.
- **Daily login streaks with resets, or expiring season rewards**: this is the FOMO the brief rules out. Bank the rewards instead.
