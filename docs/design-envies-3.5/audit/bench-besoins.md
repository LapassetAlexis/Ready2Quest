# Virtual-pet needs and mood systems: benchmark for a WoW Classic companion addon

Sourcing: points marked **(src)** come from sources found in this search. Points marked **(unverified)** are from memory and should be checked before anyone quotes them.

## 1. Tamagotchi and Nintendogs
- **Tamagotchi:** the pet calls for attention when it is hungry, unhappy or sick. If the player does not answer within about 15 minutes, that counts as a care mistake. Mistakes decide what the pet grows into, and enough of them make it die or run away. Some pets died after only a few hours of neglect. Newer models (Tamagotchi iD) leave a farewell letter instead of dying **(src)** ([Tamagotchi wiki](https://tamagotchi.fandom.com/wiki/Care), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2017-10-10-20th-anniversary-tamagotchi.html)).
- **Nintendogs:** a dog never dies, gets sick or ages. If it is neglected for weeks it may run away for a while and come back, sometimes with a gift **(src)** ([TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/Nintendogs), [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/abandoned-nintendogs-found-alive-after-almost-10-years-1798504527)).
- **Why it softened:** death mostly produced guilt and quitting. Kotaku, Gfinity and other press keep coming back to the guilt players feel about pets they abandoned. Later entries moved toward penalties that can be undone.

## 2. WoW hunter pet happiness and loyalty
- **How it worked:** happiness drained over time and dropped when the pet died. Hunters raised it with Feed Pet, using food of the pet's diet. In Classic, a happy pet dealt about 125% damage, a content one 100% and an unhappy one 75%. An unhappy pet with low loyalty could abandon the hunter **(unverified for the exact figures)**.
- **Removal:** loyalty went in patch 3.0.2. Happiness went in 4.1 (April 2011), and the happy-state damage bonus became the baseline for every pet **(src)** ([Warcraft wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Happiness)).
- **Why:** I found no official Blizzard statement explaining it. The press read it as removing a chore that added no real choice: Engadget called the removal "long overdue" and happiness a distraction **(src)** ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2011-04-28-scattered-shots-hunters-and-patch-4-1.html)).
- **Lesson:** Blizzard did not delete the bonus. It made the bonus permanent, so the reward stayed and the upkeep went away.

## 3. The Sims
- **Before Sims 4:** in Sims 1 and 2, needs decayed and low needs made Sims refuse to act. That is constant upkeep. Sims 3 moodlets mainly pushed a single happiness bar up or down **(src)** ([Sims wiki](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Moodlet)).
- **Sims 4:** moodlets are timed and stack up into one of several emotions. Each emotion unlocks actions or improves results, for example Inspired makes creative work better. Needs still exist and still feed moodlets **(src)** ([Carl's guide](https://carls-sims-4-guide.com/emotions), [Prima](https://primagames.com/eguides/the-sims-4-eguide/feelings-lots-of-feelings/moodlets)).
- **Effect:** the question the player asks changes from "what is failing?" to "what state do I want my Sim in?"

## 4. Pokémon
- **Amie and Refresh:** affection unlocks battle perks by heart level **(src)** ([Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Refresh), [Serebii](https://serebii.net/sunmoon/pokemonrefresh.shtml)):
  - 2 hearts: +20% experience
  - 3 hearts: can survive a knockout hit with 1 HP
  - 4 hearts: can dodge attacks and cure its own status
  - 5 hearts: higher critical-hit chance

  Fullness and enjoyment go down over time, but they only limit how fast affection can rise. **Affection itself never decays.** The main complaint was that the perks made the game too easy.
- **Pokémon GO buddy** **(src)** ([GO wiki](https://pokemongo.fandom.com/wiki/Buddy_Pok%C3%A9mon), [GO Hub](https://pokemongohub.net/post/user-guide/go-hub-guide-to-buddy-interactions-in-pokemon-go/), [Niantic](https://pokemongo.com/en/news/buddyadventurelaunch)):
  - **Reaching Excited:** collect 32 emotion points (walking 2 km gives 4, feeding or playing gives 2), or feed a Poffin for an instant Excited.
  - **What it gives:** candy for half the walking distance, and double hearts per activity and per daily cap (the bonus category is not doubled).
  - **Duration:** reportedly about 6 hours, but this is not confirmed.
  - **Daily cap:** about 12 hearts without Excited. Sources disagree on the maximum (20 or 32).
  - **Decay:** mood drops slowly, but no progress is ever lost.
- **Pokémon Sleep** **(src)** ([Serebii](https://serebii.net/pokemonsleep/helperpokemon.shtml), [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Sleep/archives/418668)):
  - Helpers gather berries for Snorlax, and its favourite berries give more strength.
  - Helper energy drops during the day and refills when the player sleeps.
  - Friendship comes from biscuits (+1, or +3 for the better ones) and goes up only.
  - Neglect is not possible, because playing simply means sleeping.

## 5. MMOs: framing needs as positive buffs
- **WoW rested XP** **(src)** ([Vanilla wiki](https://vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Rest), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2009/07/09/breakfast-topic-different-states-of-rest/), [Warcraft Tavern](https://warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/rested-experience)):
  - **In beta:** after a few hours of play, experience was cut to 50%, with states running from rested to exhausted. Testers hated it.
  - **The reframe:** the same numbers were presented as a bonus, 200% experience while rested.
  - **At launch:** rest builds 5% of a level per 8 hours, up to 150% (1.5 levels).
  - **Sourcing caveat:** the "same numbers seen from the opposite side" quote is fan-sourced and marked "citation needed" on the wiki.
- **Well Fed (WoW), FFXIV food and Guild Wars 2 food:**
  - These are timed buffs of 15 to 60 minutes. Not eating means no bonus, never a penalty.
  - FFXIV food also gives +3% experience **(src for the 3%)** ([GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/nplni/ffxiv-list-of-food-for-physical-damage-dealers-dddps)).
  - FFXIV rested experience builds up in sanctuaries **(unverified)**.

## 6. Stardew Valley, Animal Crossing, Neko Atsume
- **Stardew Valley** **(src)** ([wiki](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Friendship)):
  - Talking to a villager gives +20 friendship per day.
  - Ignored villagers lose 2 points per day, but decay stops at 10 hearts.
  - Dating partners lose 8 to 10 points per day.
  - A spouse loses 20 points per day and never stops decaying. Players complain that married life becomes a chore.
- **Animal Crossing: New Horizons** **(src)** ([Nookipedia](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Friendship), [datamine](https://nintendoeverything.com/animal-crossing-new-horizons-datamine-provides-details-about-how-villagers-move-out/)):
  - Not talking to villagers does not lower friendship. They only comment that you were away.
  - The chance a villager asks to move out falls as friendship rises (friendship runs 0 to 255).
  - Villager requests act as small wishes the player can fulfil.
- **Neko Atsume** **(src)** ([Crimson](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2016/2/2/neko-atsume/), [IDEO](https://medium.com/ideo-stories/neko-atsume-a-short-story-on-games-cats-addiction-206b74ca032a)):
  - An empty bowl just means fewer cats visit. There is no shaming and no death.
  - Better food attracts rarer cats.
  - It is the standard example of a game players return to without any punishment for leaving.

## 7. Companions in action games
- **Monster Hunter Palico and Palamute:** they have no needs. Players build the bond through gear and skills **(unverified)**.
- **Ni no Kuni familiars:** treats permanently raise stats, up to a fullness cap that resets. Nothing decays **(unverified)**.
- **Red Dead Redemption 2 horse** **(src)** ([Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/108131/horse-bonding-grooming-and-reviving-red-dead-redemption-2), [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/red-dead-redemption-2/guides/full-guide-looking-after-your-horse-in-red-dead-redemption-2), [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-how-to-increase-horse-bonding-level/)):
  - Bonding rises with riding, brushing, feeding, patting and calming.
  - Level 2 unlocks the rear. Level 3 unlocks the skid turn and stop. Level 4 is the maximum, unlocks the rest of the moves (drift and dressage-style moves) and gives a trophy.
  - I could not confirm which exact level gives the drift.
  - Hunger and dirt only drain the horse's health and stamina cores. Bonding effectively never drops.
- **FFXIV chocobo companion:** it levels up through battle; Curiel Root boosts its experience **(src)** ([consolegameswiki](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Chocobo_Raising)). Nothing decays, and the feeding is entirely positive.

## 8. Research: loss aversion, chores and wishes
- **Loss aversion:** players weigh a loss about twice as heavily as an equal gain (Kahneman and Tversky). Geoff Engelstein's GDC 2017 talk, "Board Game Design and the Psychology of Loss Aversion", covers framing in games **(src)** ([GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1024456/contactUs)).
- **Streaks and resets:** streaks that reset make players anxious and drive them to quit. The usual fixes are streak freezes and grace periods **(src)** ([Smashing Magazine](https://smashingmagazine.com/2026/02/designing-streak-system-ux-psychology/), [Yu-kai Chou](https://yukaichou.com/gamification-analysis/streak-design-gamification-motivation-burnout/)).
- **Spiritfarer:** each spirit's mood is the sum of named, visible factors. A favourite meal gives +2, but serving it again on following days adds nothing more. Hugs raise mood, and Elena is the one spirit who refuses them. Perks scale with mood, and the top state is "ecstatic". The whole system reads as wishes to satisfy rather than meters to protect **(src)** ([wiki](https://spiritfarer.fandom.com/wiki/Mood)).
- **Cozy Grove:** daily bear quests are capped, at about 30 to 60 minutes of play per day. Each completed quest brings colour back to the island. I found no rule penalising skipped days **(src)** ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1458100)).

## Synthesis

| Model | Attachment | Chore feeling | Readability | Late-game relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Negative decay** (Tamagotchi, Classic pet happiness, Stardew spouse) | Strong at first, then turns into guilt | High; Blizzard removed it in 4.1 | Poor: four meters, and a "half bonus" penalty is hard to read | Falls off: becomes upkeep or gets ignored |
| **Timed buff** (rested XP, Well Fed, Sims 4 emotions, Pokémon GO Excited) | Medium | Low: skipping only means no bonus | Good: one icon and a timer | Good if it is refreshed by actions players do anyway |
| **Wish or request** (Animal Crossing, Spiritfarer, Cozy Grove) | High: the companion seems to want things | Low to medium, as long as there is a cap and no punishment | Very good: one visible request at a time | Good: varied, and can tie into content |
| **Pure bond with no needs** (RDR2 levels, Amie affection, Palico, Neko Atsume) | High: progress only goes up | None | Very good | Permanent unlocks keep it relevant; needs content to stay lively |

## Recommendation for the companion
Follow Blizzard's 4.1 move:
1. Make the current bonuses the baseline. They are never halved.
2. Replace the four decaying needs with:
   - a bond that only goes up, with RDR2-style levels that unlock things;
   - one timed buff, such as an "Excited" or "Well Fed" state of about 30 to 60 minutes, triggered by feeding or playing;
   - an optional daily wish (a food, a zone, a creature to catch) with a capped reward.
3. Hunger, energy, mood and curiosity can stay as flavour only: emotes and lines the companion says, with no effect on stats.
