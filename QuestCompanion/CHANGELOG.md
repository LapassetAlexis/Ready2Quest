# QuestCompanion

## 1.0.0

**First release, split from Ready2Quest 1.26.0.** Première version, séparée de Ready2Quest 1.26.0.

- Requires **Ready2Quest 1.26.0** or later. Nécessite **Ready2Quest 1.26.0** ou plus récent.
- Coming from Ready2Quest 1.25 or earlier? Your companion, journal and chests are moved over automatically, character by character, on first login. Tu viens de Ready2Quest 1.25 ou avant ? Ta créature, ton carnet et tes coffres sont repris automatiquement, personnage par personnage, à la première connexion.

Included / Au programme : a travel companion that talks, has four needs and evolves when happy; a journal with stamps, per-zone completion (villages, bestiary, named foes, gathering, quests), zones prefilled for Era and Forever; zone chests with cosmetic rewards (appearances, ornaments, frames, backgrounds); on-screen goal tracker.

Clients : WoW Forever (1.60) and Classic Era (1.15). English and French.
